Architecture approach

Recommended Dell Technologies stack

Bridging the IT/OT divide requires infrastructure built for harsh environments but managed with enterprise IT tooling.

Environmental harshness

Dust, temperature extremes, and vibration demand ruggedised edge nodes, not repurposed data center servers.

Bandwidth constraints

Video streams for QA cannot traverse the WAN. Inference must happen at the edge, sending only metadata back.

OT team autonomy

Plant managers need reliability. Edge infrastructure must be zero-touch provisioned and remotely managed by IT.

Legacy protocol translation

Modern platforms must ingest data from decades-old PLCs (Modbus, OPC UA) without disrupting operations.
