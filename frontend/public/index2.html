Architecture approach

Recommended Dell Technologies stack

Modernising clinical care requires infrastructure that guarantees 24x7 availability and strict patient data security.

High Availability

Clinical systems (EMR) cannot go down. Redundant, highly available infrastructure is non-negotiable at the edge.

Large Data Gravity

Medical imaging (MRI/CT) generates petabytes of data. Moving this to the cloud for inference is often too slow and costly.

Strict Compliance

Patient data must be secured against ransomware and comply with stringent data privacy regulations (HIPAA/DPDP).

Distributed Footprint

Managing IT across dozens of remote clinics requires zero-touch provisioning and centralised fleet management.
